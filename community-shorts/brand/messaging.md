# Community messaging for shorts

Pulled from the internal Community skills (ICP + messaging framework, product knowledge, brand
docs). Use it to write the intro headline, the chips and the end card. Do not invent claims.

## One-line value statement

Community helps brands turn texts into conversations that take people from first-time interest
to long-term action.

## The two pillars (every short should land at least one)

1. **Every text is a data point that moves the funnel.** A like, click or open shows activity, not
   intent. On Community people reply, and that reply is explicit, current, permission-based data the
   brand owns. Use when the customer talks about surveys, asking questions, tagging replies,
   segmenting, retargeting or knowing what fans want.
2. **Know who wants to engage, and reach them directly.** No feeds, no filters, no middlemen. Reach
   does not depend on an algorithm or an inbox tab. A one-to-many text still feels one-to-one, and
   the relationship stays with the brand if a platform changes. Use when the customer talks about
   social reach dropping, email fatigue, owning the audience, or a direct line.

## Product differentiators that make good on-screen chips

Keep chips short (2 to 5 words), with an optional sub-line. Only use one when the speaker says
something that backs it up.

| Speaker says something like | Chip | Sub-line |
|---|---|---|
| people text back, replies, conversations | Two-way conversations | Replies, not just reach |
| we ask a question, survey, tag replies | Data fans give you directly | Segments built on real answers |
| we group people, target, segment | Subcommunities | Send to the fans who care |
| social reach, algorithm, nobody saw it | No algorithm in the way | Straight to their phone |
| it's ours, we own it, our list | An audience you own | It stays with your brand |
| welcome texts, automations, flows | Flows that run on their own | Welcome, follow-up, birthday |
| keywords, QR codes, sign-up links | Growth tools that fill the list | Keywords, links, QR codes |
| personal, first name, feels 1:1 | Feels one-to-one at scale | Dynamic fields, real replies |

Official names: say Subcommunities (not tags or segments) when naming the feature on screen.
Flows, Keywords, Campaign Surveys, Growth Tools and Insights are the other official names.

## Proof rules

- On-screen numbers come from the speaker's own words in the footage. If they say "about thirty
  percent", the card can say 30% with a label that matches what they said.
- Anything else (a number they did not say on camera) must come from the
  `community-case-studies` skill, which holds published, cleared metrics. Never round up, never
  combine numbers, never guess.
- A feature description is not proof. Do not put "98% open rate" style claims on screen unless a
  case study backs them.

## Voice

- Sentence case for every headline. No trailing punctuation.
- Plain, confident, warm. Short words.
- Say "fans", "members", "customers" or "audience" to match the speaker's world (music and sports
  say fans, retail says customers, politics says supporters).
- Do not name competitors on screen.
